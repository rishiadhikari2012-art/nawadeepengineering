Step-By-Step Process

Hassle-Free Municipality Naksa Pass Guide

We handle the entire municipal building permit and D-PIMS registration from document preparation to obtaining the final Nirman Sampanna certificate.

Step 01

Land & Legal Verification

We review your Lalpurja (Land Ownership Certificate), Cadastral Trace Map, Tiropani tax receipts, and ensure compliance with municipal setbacks and Right-of-Way (RoW).

Step 02

2D/3D Architectural Drawing

Preparation of municipal standard floor plans, elevations, sections, site plan, plumbing, electrical, and drainage drawings strictly following Nepal National Building Codes.

Step 03

Structural Design & ETABS Report

For buildings above 2 storeys or commercial complexes, our licensed structural engineers perform complete earthquake seismic analysis under NBC 105:2020.

Step 04

D-PIMS Online Submission

We upload the complete architectural and structural packages to the Municipality's online D-PIMS system and manage municipal scrutiny seamlessly.

Step 05

Site Verification & Plinth Approval

Coordination with municipal field inspectors for site inspection, boundary verification, and obtaining the Plinth Level (Tala Thap) construction permit.

Step 06

Completion Certificate (Nirman Sampanna)

Post-construction inspection assistance to obtain the official Nirman Sampanna Pramanpatra (Building Completion Certificate) for banking and legal validity.
